Why Study 1 models appear here (In-Context Branch): Rather than retraining weights under a pit-free contract (Study 2 Stage B), Study 3 keeps three frozen Study 1 models (kev-0.8b, self-jev-minilm, and frontier-llm) under the original pits-enabled RaceEnv contract. The Z arm records their zero-shot closed-loop baseline on seed_B (2001–2005), while A and F test whether prior-race history lets them recover without weight updates.
